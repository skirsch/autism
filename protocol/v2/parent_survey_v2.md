**Q0.4 — Developmental pattern (Case Definition)**
> Which of the following best describes your child's early development?
> 1. They never met early milestones (e.g., no meaningful speech, no shared attention).
> 2. **SORA Pattern:** They met milestones, then experienced a sudden departure from their prior baseline within **five days or less** (e.g., rapid loss of words/eye contact, new repetitive behaviors, or marked sensory changes).
> 3. They met milestones, then **gradually** lost skills or behaviors over a period of **months**.
> 4. They met milestones, then **stopped making progress** (plateaued) without a clear loss.
> 5. I'm not sure.

*Routing: Option 2 → SORA path (Section 1). Options 1, 3, 4 → Non-SORA path (Section 5).*

## Section 1 — Onset Anchor (SORA Path Only)

**Q1.1 — SORA definition verification**
> You indicated your child experienced a sudden change. To confirm this fits the SORA definition, did this change:
> - Occur within a window of five days or less? (Yes/No)
> - Persist for at least four weeks? (Yes/No)
> - *If No to either, you may be redirected to the non-SORA section.*

**Q1.2 — Onset timing**
> Please provide the approximate date this change began: [Month/Year] [Day (if known)]. 
> How confident are you in this date? (Very/Somewhat/Not confident)

**Q1.3 — Anchor source**
> What helped you determine this date? (Vaccine card, medical note, photo/video, calendar, memory, family event)

**Q1.4 — Change domains (Check all that apply)**
> **Loss of behaviors:** Eye contact, response to name, affection, social interest, joint attention, imitation, interactive play.
> **Loss of skills:** Words, gestures, self-help, play skills, motor abilities.
> **New behaviors:** Repetitive movements, self-injury, toe walking, head banging, unusual posturing, arching, repetitive vocalizations, marked withdrawal.
> **Sensory changes:** Sensitivity to sound, light, touch, textures, smell, or food.

## Section 3 — Five-Day Pre-Onset Checklist (Prompted)

**Q3.1 — Events in the 5 days before onset**
> Thinking specifically about the **five days before** the change began, did any of the following occur?
> - **Medical:** Vaccination, Fever, Illness (describe), Medication (describe), Acetaminophen (Tylenol) or ibuprofen, Antibiotics, Hospital/ER visit, Seizure.
> - **Life events:** Geographic move, New sibling, Started daycare/school, Family stress.
> - **Negative controls:** Dental visit, Well-child checkup (no vaccines), Holiday, Birthday.
