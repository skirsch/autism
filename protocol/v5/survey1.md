# Partner member survey and research-interest opt-in

**Version:** 1.7 draft
**Date:** October 4, 2026
**Status:** Proposed referral arrangement; partner questionnaire is independently controlled; study recruitment requires approval
**Controlling design:** [study_overview.md](study_overview.md)

## Scope and custody

“Survey 1” is retained as a stage label, not a study-authored research instrument. The partner may ask its own audience questions to learn about members under its own privacy/retention terms and applicable oversight. We do not dictate its full questionnaire or require our research-consent form on that independent survey. Whether its actual activities constitute research is not settled merely by calling it a member survey. If jointly designed to collect data for our research, return for a revised oversight determination before use.

The partner keeps all individual survey answers and email addresses. The study does not receive or analyze individual partner answers, including recalled onset ages; they are not part of our child-level research dataset. Separately permitted, privacy-reviewed aggregate summaries may be used to describe selection across stages, subject to appropriate determination. Study consent occurs on our Survey 2 landing page before any research answers or study-held contact details.

## Suggested final question — permission for partner contact

**May we contact you about research studies relevant to your child, including studies that may offer payment for participation?**

- Yes.
- No.

If Yes, explain: “We may send you invitations to consider relevant research studies. This does not enroll you in a study or require you to answer questions or provide records. We will keep your contact details and survey answers with us; this permission does not authorize sharing them with research teams. You may ask us to stop sending these invitations at any time.”

Use the partner's actual contact/privacy/retention notice and opt-out method. No response or No does not authorize research invitations. Do not promise payment merely for joining this interest list; any later invitation explains that study's actual payment conditions. Do not describe these invitations as studies that will benefit the child: no direct benefit is promised. This is contact permission, not research consent, parental/adult-child authority or medical-record authorization.

## Later study-specific invitations

The partner independently maintains its member survey and research-interest list, keeps all individual answers and email addresses, and forwards our IRB-approved study invitation itself. The study receives no partner contact list, individual answers or person-linked selection flags. Before study invitations, document a cause-neutral relevance rule and its mapping to the partner's available questions; include possible/uncertain cases without ranking by vaccination, presumed cause, age spikes, date precision or records-confidence. Partner-recalled ages are referral information, not verified study measurements. Request safe aggregate selection/invitation counts where available and label missing denominators. Parents voluntarily follow a non-person-identifying source/batch link to our consent page; no research answers or study-held contact details are collected before affirmative consent. The study collects contact details directly afterward only with the parent's permission. The partner sends initial invitations and one approved day-7 reminder, honoring its opt-outs; study staff send later records requests only with study-held contact permission.

The partner's general opt-in is permission for that partner to send relevant research invitations, not research consent or permission to share contacts/records. No study research-consent form is attached to the independent member survey under this proposed model. This does not determine that the partner survey is nonresearch: its actual purpose, analysis and planned uses must support an appropriate independent oversight determination. Submit our recruitment plan, relevance mapping and actual study-specific invitation to IPAKIRB; ask whether it also needs the partner opt-in/notice or questionnaire to assess the arrangement. Partner authorship or a pre-existing list does not itself establish exemption or approval.

A jointly agreed, versioned relevance mapping identifies opted-in families reporting a possible identifiable lasting change after apparently normal development. Include unsure/possible reports. Do not use partner age peaks, vaccine dates, vaccine opinions or evidence-confidence to select invitees. Exact wording and available source fields may differ; disclose differences and limitations. If the partner's existing categories exclude relevant phenotypes (for example, a mandatory seven-day cutoff or skill-loss-only question), document the mismatch and broaden the cause-neutral invitation route where feasible rather than silently claiming complete coverage.

Use approved study-specific messages in [recruitment_and_compensation.md](recruitment_and_compensation.md). The partner forwards the invitation and any approved reminder; parents choose whether to visit our study page. A source/batch link must not encode an email, child details or an individual partner response. No identifying referral tokens or tracking pixels are required to transfer a roster. Lawfully handle/minimize unavoidable web/security metadata separately; visits are not consent and delivery is not evidence of reading.


### Aggregate comparison across recruitment stages

Where the partner's permissions, privacy review and IPAKIRB determination permit, request only non-identifying aggregate summaries of its survey answers, alongside aggregate opt-in, relevance-selection and invitation counts. Compare comparable variables across the partner-survey respondents, consenting study respondents and records-submitting/validated subsets; keep the 100-record target distinct from achieved counts. Specify the question wording, age units/bins, unknowns, source/batch, time period, child-selection rule and denominator for every summary. Suppress small or identifying cells and avoid overlapping tables that reveal individuals. No individual partner answers, emails or linkage keys are imported. These comparisons describe recruitment/selection and recall differences, not population representativeness, individual verification or causation. Different questions, denominators or ascertainment may prevent direct comparison; report that rather than silently treating stages as equivalent. Do not alter confidence waves to match partner age peaks. Any planned analysis of partner aggregates is a disclosed research use, not automatically outside oversight because the survey is partner-owned. Obtain the appropriate determination/permission before acquisition or use; availability is a dependency, not a guaranteed dataset.
