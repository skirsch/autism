## Partner screen privacy and partner-contact permission

The partner independently maintains its member survey and research-interest list, keeps all individual answers and email addresses, and forwards our IRB-approved study invitation itself. The study receives no partner contact list, individual answers or person-linked selection flags. Before study invitations, document a cause-neutral relevance rule and its mapping to the partner's available questions; include possible/uncertain cases without ranking by vaccination, presumed cause, age spikes, date precision or records-confidence. Partner-recalled ages are referral information, not verified study measurements. Request safe aggregate selection/invitation counts where available and label missing denominators. Parents voluntarily follow a non-person-identifying source/batch link to our consent page; no research answers or study-held contact details are collected before affirmative consent. The study collects contact details directly afterward only with the parent's permission. The partner sends initial invitations and one approved day-7 reminder, honoring its opt-outs; study staff send later records requests only with study-held contact permission.

Proposed final partner question: “May we contact you about research studies relevant to your child, including studies that may offer payment for participation?” Yes permits invitations sent by the partner; No or no response does not. The partner retains emails and answers and does not disclose them to us. This is not study research consent or permission to obtain medical records.

Explain that the partner itself sends relevant invitations, participation is voluntary and its opt-out/privacy/retention terms apply. No commitment to participate or provide records, and no automatic payment, is implied. Our approved study-specific invitation provides required truthful study/funder information without naming candidate events.

The partner's general opt-in is permission for that partner to send relevant research invitations, not research consent or permission to share contacts/records. No study research-consent form is attached to the independent member survey under this proposed model. This does not determine that the partner survey is nonresearch: its actual purpose, analysis and planned uses must support an appropriate independent oversight determination. Submit our recruitment plan, relevance mapping and actual study-specific invitation to IPAKIRB; ask whether it also needs the partner opt-in/notice or questionnaire to assess the arrangement. Partner authorship or a pre-existing list does not itself establish exemption or approval.

### Privacy retention and public narratives

The risks include distress from recall, unauthorized access and recognition of a family from a detailed account. Restricting access and removing identifying details reduces but does not eliminate those risks. All submitted answers and uploaded materials are proposed for encrypted restricted retention for 20 years after study closure, or longer if required. Qualified researchers may review restricted materials only under the approved access process and applicable confidentiality/data-use terms.

We plan to publish coded data and carefully redacted versions of your written accounts, including saved partial accounts, if they can be made sufficiently safe. Uploaded source materials, contact information and absolute identifying dates will not be public. We may generalize or withhold a narrative or row if it remains identifying. The public dataset will be deposited on Zenodo independently of whether a journal accepts a paper or any pattern appears. The public data and permitted written accounts will be shared under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, allowing reuse and adaptations, including commercial reuse, with attribution and notice of changes. Attribution will identify the dataset, not you. Public copies may persist indefinitely. We cannot guarantee recall or deletion of downloaded copies.

By agreeing, you permit the described redaction and public licensing of the written material you supply to the extent you hold those rights. This is not a transfer of copyright or permission to publish uploaded source documents. You may choose not to submit material you are not authorized to share. Third-party information must be handled under the approved privacy process; parental participation does not establish permission to publish another person's private information.

You may stop future participation or contact at any time without loss of benefits you otherwise receive. Already submitted material remains retained and usable under the proposed policy, including when you decline the later part. You may report an error or privacy concern; corrections are preserved alongside originals, and privacy incidents will be addressed. No statement in this form waives your legal rights. Applicable law and the approved protocol govern any rights that cannot be limited by this policy.

Payment does not depend on a particular answer, timing pattern or reviewer decision.

## IRB determinations requested

1. Whether adults and children/adult children whose identifiable private records are received are research subjects; appropriate parental permission, adult-subject consent and child assent requirements or justified waivers. No direct child interaction does not alone resolve this determination.
2. Review category and risk determination for a linked, identifiable-source design; do not assert exemption or anonymity.
3. Whether withholding named candidate events until after the locked account constitutes an alteration of consent. If applicable, consider 45 CFR 46.116(f), including minimal risk, impracticability, necessity of identifiable information, rights/welfare and appropriate additional information. The priming rationale is proposed, not proof every criterion is met. If the IRB does not approve, do not deploy; obtain a study-team design decision.
4. Separate approval of pre-disclosure partial retention/public narrative use, 20-year restricted retention and the proposed no-deletion-after-submission policy. Do not justify nondeletion by claiming linked records cannot be located.
5. Approved electronic consent documentation, copy provision and any waiver of signed documentation under 46.117; click-through capability alone is not an approved waiver.
6. Whether sponsor/interest disclosure and participant narrative licensing are sufficient; lawful permissions and withdrawal rights override unapproved template wording.
