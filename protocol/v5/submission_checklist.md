## External facts required for a complete submission

- [ ] Brian's PI acceptance, exact credentials/affiliation/title, CV, address/contact, eligibility and required training; other team roles/training/current individual interest declarations.
- [ ] CHD amount/recipient/mechanism/budget, actual role and contract terms, accepted result-independent release commitment, payments/in-kind support and conflict-management determination.
- [ ] Actual participant study mailbox/backup and independent IRB rights contact; measured completion-time estimates and confirmed payment-delivery timing.
- [ ] IPAK's current required form/process and acceptance of current Part A; supplied official verbatim liability/indemnification and non-accountability statements and required signatures.
- [ ] Intended study calendar/enrollment end/closure and support continuity; current fees/actual budgets rather than historic template estimates.
- [ ] Custodian acceptance, actual systems/regions/vendors/metadata, confirmed security/key/backup/incident/access/retention and source re-review procedures.
- [ ] Each actual source's authorization/identity and source-specific invitation denominator, custody/contact permission/secure handoff/reimbursement/site arrangements in restricted attachments.
- [ ] Lawful geography/current child or adult subject authority; IRB review-category/child protections and exact staged-consent, partial/public-use, retention/nondeletion and electronic documentation determinations.

Some approvals may be issued as review conditions rather than pre-application facts; record their actual status and do not mark them resolved prematurely. A missing official statement/signature or PI/contact fact cannot be filled with invented text.

## Document authority and corrections

Overview fixes the design; consent file supplies proposed participant terms; specialized manuals implement those terms. Survey files retain substantive questions and point to the complete consent/screen contract, not obsolete shortened notices. If two files disagree, do not choose the one that produces a desired dataset: flag conflict, amend and version the complete package before use. Institutional requirements control approvals; changes to fixed choices require study-team decisions, not quiet overrides.

The README/guide and work log link this package. Older protocol versions and history are not altered or substituted for this design. No commit, public dataset deposit, external message, recruitment or IRB submission is part of drafting these files.
