## Core measures preserved for later analysis

Use one **ONSET date**: either parent's first notice of the qualifying lasting change. Preserve the original report, later clarifications, each reviewer's assessment, accepted value, corroboration, and precision separately. Earlier illness/uncertainty is timeline context, not another onset endpoint; earliest documented post-change date is a documentation field.

- **Age:** ONSET minus parent-entered birthdate, in days and completed months.
- **A:** ONSET minus the selected most recent actual vaccination date on or before ONSET within 90 days.
- **B:** total complete normal days in that vaccination-to-ONSET window, including disconnected periods, using observed pre-vaccination behavior/skills as baseline. Count vaccination day only if normal throughout; count through the day before ONSET, never ONSET day or pre-vaccination days. Known values satisfy 0 ≤ B ≤ A; A = 0 implies B = 0.
- **C:** ONSET minus the most recent eligible calendar-quarter start or fixed nominal vaccination-age grid date on or before ONSET; nominal grid dates follow the same 90-day convention. The same 2-, 4-, 6-, 12-, 15- and 18-month grid applies to every child without birth-year customization, as agreed under Q21.

Without an eligible vaccination anchor, A/B are not applicable, not zero. Keep normal, non-normal, unassessable and unremembered day statuses separate. Preserve exact, approximate, lower-bound, unknown, and baseline-unestablished answers and the supporting timeline. B is directly reported, not derived by subtracting symptom duration from A. Raw B > A discrepancies remain visible and prompt clarification, never silent clamping.

Preserve C values with reference-system/grid-rule version, nominal ages, calendar arithmetic, precision, eligibility, and missing/no-anchor flags. The fixed age grid is a constructed benchmark inspired by common vaccination ages, not a reconstruction of each child's historical CDC schedule, administered products, or biological timing. Nominal dates never replace actual administration. Additional fixed-day grids and shifted references are optional later descriptive uses, not additional pre-disclosure prompts or formal tests required in the data paper.

## Data layers and unit of observation

The post-disclosure vaccination-to-ONSET narrative must include the normal periods counted in B, not just notable symptoms/events. Preserve each described period's dates or duration, baseline behavior/skill observations, observer, and normal/non-normal/unassessable/unknown status when available. Retain all disconnected normal periods. Cross-check the directly reported B count against this timeline, recording discrepancies and later clarifications without overwriting raw answers or the locked original narrative. Silence about symptoms does not establish normal functioning. This prompted chronology covers the full vaccination-to-ONSET window even when it exceeds the original unprompted 15-day narrative.

1. **Recruitment-source log:** One row per source and invitation batch, with source code, attempted/delivered/undeliverable counts, batch and reminder dates, confidence group, and stage counts where available. Do not infer that delivery means an invitation was read. Partner identities may be described in the regulatory file but are not embedded in respondent-facing Survey 1.
2. **Partner referral/contact record:** Only an authorized email, study-assigned referral ID and administrative source/batch/permission attestation are transferred. Partner individual screen responses remain partner-held under its policy and are not research/public rows. Request available aggregate invitation/screen/referral counts; document missing counts.
3. **Survey 2 response:** One immutable study response per submission, linked to its authorized referral, study invitation and confirmed child when known. Every authorized possible-case referral may respond, including best estimates/low confidence/unknowns. Preserve A1, neutral A2 date-confidence/A3 timing/A4 records-confidence, disclosure state and later prompted answers separately. Parent confidence is not date verification. Partial answers remain data, not negative answers; response and unique-child counts are separate.
4. **Evidence inventory:** One row per submitted item, linked to the child through a restricted identifier. Record media type, receipt time, claimed event date, file-created date when available, provenance, content summary, authenticity/legibility concerns, and restricted storage pointer. The source file itself is never public.
5. **Reviewer assessment:** One immutable row per reviewer per child, with completion time, SORA judgment, each date/bound/status, source citation, evidence tier, and conflict flags. Each reviewer locks one assessment without access to the other two judgments. Corrections, if allowed for a clerical error, require a separately logged amendment; the original is not overwritten.
6. **Accepted case table:** One row per child, retaining parent-reported, reviewer-specific, and accepted values separately. A value survives only when at least two reviewers independently choose the exact same value or status; otherwise it is unresolved. Derived intervals are calculated only from the accepted inputs they require. The table carries separate administrative, scientific, evidence, and public-release flags.
7. **Public-use files:** De-identified case and technical-validation tables, a carefully redacted narrative file when safe, a dictionary, flow counts, transformation code, and a release manifest. No contact information, absolute dates, source files, or direct identifiers are released. Restricted originals and the mapping key remain under controlled access.

Stable internal child and evidence identifiers will be randomly assigned; public identifiers will be different and nonreversible without the restricted crosswalk. No respondent name or email may serve as an analysis key. Preserve raw answers and an auditable correction history instead of replacing them with adjudicated values.

## Minimum dictionary and provenance fields

The field-level implementation dictionary will give every variable a name, type, units, allowed values, missing codes, collection screen or source item, transformation rule, and public/restricted disposition. These groups are required:

| Group | Fields and distinctions that must survive |
| --- | --- |
| Funnel | Aggregate partner screen/invitation counts where available; authorized email referrals; separate Survey 2 invitation/consent/screen/A1-lock/A2–A4/disclosure states; records-request group/batch/deadline/receipt; payment status. No individual partner screening answers are transferred. |
| Child and phenotype | Survey 2 parent-entered birthdate, selected-child confirmation, reported phenotype/onset age, date confidence and A4 records-confidence; parent-reported diagnosis; loss/reduction/new-behavior flags. Low confidence does not block survey access; parent report is not documented diagnosis. |
| Narratives and sequence | Original locked unprompted A1 text/timestamp; cause-neutral A3 uncertain-period and return assessment; prompted full-window chronology; pre-vaccination behavior/skill baseline and dateability; direct total normal-day B count and all supporting normal periods, including disconnected stretches. Preserve symptom timing, maintained/returned/no-return/partial-day sequence status, non-normal versus unassessable/unremembered days, count precision and discrepancies. Symptom resolution and silence do not establish normal functioning; later answers never overwrite A1. |
| Timing | Parent-first-notice ONSET, clarifications and plausible bounds; each reviewer's ONSET assessment, bounds, earliest documented post-change date and unresolved statuses; exact two-of-three accepted values; citations and evidence tiers. Earlier illness and uncertain periods are contextual timeline fields, not another onset date. Preserve skill/behavior observation bounds separately. |
| Visits and products | Parent-reported most recent vaccination within 90 days before ONSET, actual administration date/products when documented, later-vaccination status, prior-visit spacing, vaccine-free regular well visit, no-visit status, lifetime vaccination report, 90-day history-completeness status, and disagreement flags. A scheduled dose is not an administered dose. |
| Evidence and review | Item type, provenance, content/date support, record usability, reviewer citations, agreement class (3/3, 2/3, unresolved), and reasons for unresolved judgments. An after-only item can establish a documented post-change observation without proving the first onset day. |
| Derived variables | Age in days/completed months at accepted ONSET; A from the eligible vaccination; directly reported B, count precision and sequence/timeline support; C for calendar-quarter/nominal CDC anchors with rule version, eligibility/no-anchor and missing-input flags; ONSET and vaccination weekday where exact dates permit. Visit-to-ONSET lag for vaccine-free well visits and vaccination-to-documentation lag are separately labeled; neither changes ONSET or supplies A/B in no-vaccination cases. |
| Release | Consent version and data-sharing permission, public-use eligibility, narrative publication/suppression decision, case-row publication/suppression decision, reason category, redaction version, and reviewer signoff. These flags must not depend on the direction or strength of a timing result. |

Use explicit missing/status values: **not asked due to branch**, **not reached because of partial response**, **answered unknown**, **not supplied**, **submitted but unusable**, **reviewers unresolved**, **not applicable**, and **withheld from public release**. Do not combine these with a substantive No or zero. Exact codes and data types are implementation items to finalize with R03 and R06 before enrollment.

## Fixed construction rules

- **Agreed Q24 — duplicate-response compensation:** Normally provide one full-response compensation entitlement per confirmed child, including any applicable retroactive top-up under the approved adaptive schedule. Repeat submissions, uploads, corrections or clarifications do not create additional payments. Disclose this child-level rule before participation/records work. If both parents were separately invited and already promised payment, honor those commitments rather than retroactively penalizing duplicate invitations. Preserve the existing guaranteed good-faith payment commitments; do not claw back promised/issued compensation or make payment depend on reviewer conclusions. Track payee/submission/child links, amount promised, amount issued, top-ups, duplicate handling and any honored prior commitment separately in restricted records. Unconfirmed duplicate matches do not justify automatic payment denial. The operational procedure must reconcile the approved participant-facing terms and contact/payee handling without publishing payment identifiers.
- **Agreed Q23 — duplicate child submissions:** Link confirmed submissions about the same child into one child-level case, including submissions by different parents or through different recruitment sources. Preserve all original accounts, timestamps, evidence, disclosure states, respondent/source links and disagreements without blending narratives or overwriting dates. Use restricted matching identifiers and, when necessary, clarification to confirm identity; similar dates/phenotypes alone do not justify a merge. Retain suspected-but-unconfirmed duplicates with an explicit pending flag and report their uncertainty, rather than claiming they are confirmed unique cases or silently merging them. A confirmed child counts at most once toward the 100-record administrative target and once in each applicable plot, while response/source-level funnel counts remain auditable. Duplicate linkage does not change scientific or evidence judgments or discard submitted data. Recalculate unique-child administrative counts when duplicates are confirmed and log the correction; retain the original submission counts separately. Each review packet includes the linked accounts and conflicts, not a selected favorable account. Exact matching, confirmation, unresolved-duplicate and payment handling procedures remain to be completed under R02/R04/R06/R07/R10.
- **Agreed Q22 — country-level context:** In Survey 2 after disclosure, collect the child's country of residence at ONSET and, when an eligible vaccination is identified, country of residence at that vaccination, allowing different countries or unknown answers. Request no city or address. Keep vaccination-country not-applicable status separate from unknown. These are residence fields, not independently established administration locations; travel must not be silently inferred or ruled out. Do not customize the fixed nominal-age grid by country or treat it as a reconstruction of a country's recommendations. Preserve raw answers and standardize country codes in the dictionary. Any public country field or country/other-field combination remains subject to privacy review and generalization/suppression if identifying.
- **Agreed Q20 — uniform nominal-age reference:** Use one consistent, birthdate-based nominal vaccination-age grid for all children rather than customizing it to their birth-year schedule. Freeze and version the ages and date-construction rules before producing this reference. The intention is a consistent age reference across children, not a claim that biology has identical timing in every child or that this benchmark guarantees a flat no-effect histogram. Do not assign vaccine products or actual administration from the grid. Q21 specifies the agreed ages; historical schedule reconstruction is not a core C implementation requirement.
- **Agreed Q21 — exact nominal ages and unchanged eligibility:** Construct grid dates at 2, 4, 6, 12, 15 and 18 calendar months after the parent-entered birthdate. Select the most recent grid date on or before ONSET within 90 calendar days, including a coincident date as day 0; otherwise retain no qualifying anchor, without adding a 9-, 21- or 24-month point or falling back to an older out-of-window point. Month arithmetic is anchored to the original birthdate; if the target month lacks that day number, use its last calendar day and document/test the rule. The detailed cohort remains 6 through 24 completed months at ONSET, inclusive; infants below 6 months do not enter the focused evidence-supported cohort. All authorized possible-case referrals may complete Survey 2, including uncertain or out-of-range reports; The 2/4-month reference points do not broaden cohort eligibility. Out-of-range study-collected Survey 2 responses remain retained under the approved terms; later age discrepancies remain separate from administrative completion/payment.
- **Agreed Q7 — independent B assessment:** Preserve the parent's original B count/status, count clarification and timeline; each of the three reviewers independently assesses B from that timeline and available evidence using the same vaccination-to-ONSET window, baseline and complete-day rules. Reviewers remain blinded to one another's assessments and lock their answers independently. Store each reviewer's numeric value or lower/upper bounds, precision/status, uncertainty, supporting citations and conflicts separately. The accepted reviewer-assessed B value or status requires exact agreement by at least two reviewers; each bound is accepted separately under the same rule, then checked for internal consistency and consistency with accepted A. If required values/statuses do not agree or are inconsistent, retain the unresolved flag instead of averaging, clamping or manufacturing an exact number. Preserve parent-versus-reviewer differences without overwriting the parent's answer. Reviewer-assessed B is not automatically objectively verified merely because reviewers agree; report what the evidence supports. Detailed form fields and test cases remain R06/R03 work.
- **Agreed Q5 — evidence dates versus claimed event dates and conflicts:** For each supporting item preserve its creation/recording date, the observation/event date it describes when different, and the parent's claimed ONSET date, certainty assessment and plausible bounds. Preserve date provenance/precision and verification separately. For example, an item recorded two days after claimed ONSET keeps both dates and the parent's reported two-day relationship; it does not move ONSET to the evidence date or automatically verify the earlier date. Keep original pre-disclosure answers locked; after disclosure, parents may consult records and submit dates alongside their evidence, with subsequent corrections separately logged. A documentation delay alone is not a contradiction. For a material conflict, request clarification before reviewer assessments are locked; preserve both accounts and the response. If unresolved, retain the case and flag the affected date as disputed, not verified merely because two reviewers agree. Exact-day corroborated subsets cannot silently treat disputed dates as verified.
- **Agreed Q6 — clarification schedule:** Send one consolidated clarification request per case, with a single reminder seven calendar days after sending and a response window ending 14 calendar days after sending. Log request, reminder, response, deadline and nonresponse statuses separately. Incorporate timely clarifications into the shared review packet before reviewers lock assessments. Unanswered cases remain retained with unresolved/conflict flags; do not infer an answer from silence or exclude the submission from administrative counts/payment based on nonresponse. Preserve late clarifications separately without silently replacing locked reviews; any later amendment requires an auditable process, not agreement-manufacturing.
- **Agreed Q4 — verification criteria:** A vaccination date is verified only when a credible dated administration record identifies actual administration on that date, not merely a scheduled appointment or recommended dose. ONSET is verified only to the precision supported by credible contemporaneous evidence that the qualifying change was first noticed by a parent on the stated date or within stated bounds. Record the relevant evidence content, its creation date, the date it describes, and provenance; a later record repeating retrospective recollection is not contemporaneous corroboration. An after-only video or other dated post-change item establishes presence by that date and may support the separate earliest documented post-change date, but does not alone verify first notice. Only if its content or other contemporaneous evidence specifically supports first notice can it additionally support ONSET. Reviewer agreement alone does not establish verification. Verification of one date does not verify the other or establish that the submitted vaccination is the most recent in the window; history completeness remains separate. Exact evidence-tier codes and reviewer workflow remain R06 implementation work.
- **Agreed Q3 — date-specific verification and uncertainty:** For vaccination and ONSET independently, store the original reported date, any clarification, accepted date or unresolved status, precision (exact day / approximate range or week/month / unknown), verification status, source citation/evidence tier, and earliest/latest plausible bounds with bound-missing flags. Verification is distinct from precision: an exact parent report can be unverified, and evidence can corroborate a range without establishing an exact day. Do not fill missing bounds with zero uncertainty or invent a midpoint date. Parent confidence and reviewer agreement alone do not establish verification. Preserve each reviewer's assessment and the accepted value separately. Keep all cases; approximate/unresolved dates contribute only to summaries their information supports, not exact-day histogram entries or definite day-0–2/day-0–5 classifications unless resolved. Report uncertain-window classifications separately. Exact unverified dates remain explicitly labeled parent-report and distinct from corroborated-date subsets. The verification criteria are fixed in Q4; evidence-tier codes and operationalization remain R06 work.
- **Agreed Q2 — complete normal days only:** B counts calendar days at the observed pre-vaccination behavior/skill baseline throughout the day, judged from ordinary parental observations, not continuous surveillance. Preserve partial-day return status, approximate dates/duration, and uncertainty separately; do not add fractions or round into B. A definite non-normal part prevents classification as a complete normal day; an unassessable part alone does not establish abnormality. B = 0 does not imply no brief return. A normal period spanning only parts of two calendar days is not a complete calendar day; retain it as timeline context.
- **Agreed Q1 — incomplete day classification:** When A and the pre-vaccination baseline are established, preserve N = definitely complete normal days, D = definitely not complete normal days, and U = unknown/unassessable days within the same A-day window. Keep inability to assess and inability to remember as separate reasons within U. Reconciled counts satisfy N + D + U = A; raw inconsistencies remain visible and require clarification, not forced arithmetic. If U = 0 and counts are otherwise exact, B = N is exact. If U > 0, N is only a lower bound for B, with possible range N through N + U; unknown days are not abnormal days. Preserve the direct parent count separately from any reconciled count/bounds and their provenance. Approximate dates/counts retain approximate status; no applicable anchor or unestablished baseline does not produce a numeric zero. Lower-bound B values do not enter exact-count histogram bins as if exact.
- Preserve normal-period dates/durations, observed baseline behavior/skills and observers throughout the vaccination-to-ONSET window, alongside non-normal, unassessable and unknown periods. Vaccination itself is not an observed abnormality. Keep observations separate from attributed causes and reconcile the direct B answer with the narrative without overwriting raw responses.
- B adds all complete normal days within the selected vaccination-to-ONSET window, including disconnected normal periods. Exclude ONSET day and pre-vaccination days; include vaccination day only if normal throughout. Known values obey 0 ≤ B ≤ A. Preserve raw out-of-range counts and clarification history; do not clamp them. Keep zero, unknown, lower bounds and baseline-unestablished distinct; lower bounds are not exact histogram entries. No eligible vaccination anchor means A/B not applicable.
- All authorized possible-case referrals receive Survey 2 invitations without age/date-confidence/records-confidence screening. Preserve receipt-time Survey 2 reported phenotype/6–24-month eligibility separately from later reviewer-assessed eligibility. Do not revise the administrative target count because of a scientific judgment.
- The 100-submission administrative flag requires receipt-time Survey 2 reported phenotype/age eligibility, required answers and an apparently in-window vaccination or vaccine-free well-visit document. It is separate from payment, scientific usability and public release. Records-request phases, not Survey 2 access, use the original neutral A4 confidence response; exposure fields are excluded from the request-selection queue. Keep request denominators distinct from survey invitations. All requested submissions are retained beyond target.
- ONSET defines the actual 90-calendar-day visit lookback. Link at most one eligible visit to a case: the most recent documented administered vaccination on or before ONSET within that window; only if no such vaccination occurred, the most recent qualifying vaccine-free regular well visit, provided the 90-day vaccination history is sufficiently established. Otherwise code no qualifying/confirmed anchor and retain the case. A parent-reported or uncertain most-recent visit remains visible but must not be represented as confirmed.
- Derive A only from accepted ONSET and its eligible actual vaccination date; day 0 means dates coincide. Retain earlier illnesses or uncertain periods separately without converting them into negative-onset lags. Never replace an unresolved ONSET with vaccination, a nominal reference, or the first dated post-change observation.
- Preserve parent report, each locked reviewer answer, accepted answer, evidence tier, and uncertainty bounds separately. Do not average nonmatching dates or require a visit anchor to retain a child's phenotype and timing data. Unresolved/missing-date cases remain in counts and in any analysis for which their available fields suffice.
- Treat parent-reported never-vaccinated, parent-reported no vaccination in 90 days, record-supported vaccine-free-at-visit, and sufficiently established vaccine-free 90-day history as distinct states. The narrow record request does not verify a complete lifetime or 90-day history merely by obtaining one visit record.

R02 will state exact inclusion flags for each eventual descriptive subset; R06 will define evidence tiers and date-corroboration thresholds. Those rules must be agreed before review or outcome-linked analysis. This document does not create a new requirement for parents to submit full vaccination histories.

## Public release and suppression rule

**Agreed Q19 — corrections and privacy incidents:** Correct released data through a new version and a public, nonidentifying change log rather than silently overwriting the dataset. Record the affected version, issue category, correction date and changes, retaining restricted audit/provenance and prior-version relationships where safe. Corrected releases undergo the same validation and two-person privacy signoff as initial releases. For accidental disclosure, the release owner coordinates promptly with the IPAK incident contact to restrict affected files where technically possible, request repository assistance when needed, and follow the confirmed IPAK incident and applicable notification procedures. Do not delay containment for a routine correction cycle or copy identifying details into a public change log. Preserve incident evidence in restricted storage. Removal/restriction of repository files cannot guarantee removal of already downloaded copies. Specific contacts, response steps, permissions, notification requirements and repository correction/restriction capabilities must be documented and tested before deposit; this rule does not establish that they are already configured.
**Agreed Q18 — two-person privacy signoff:** Every public release, including corrected versions, requires documented privacy/disclosure-risk signoff by two authorized people, at least one of whom did not prepare that release's redactions. They review the actual complete proposed package, including narratives, derived metrics, individual reviewer fields, metadata and potentially identifying combinations or linkages, not just removal of direct identifiers. Record checker identities/roles, package version/checksum, issues and resolution, suppression/generalization decisions, and dated signoff. Do not publish when either checker identifies an unresolved privacy concern; resolve it or withhold unsafe material under the agreed suppression rules. This separation is independent of the three-reviewer scientific voting process and does not imply the privacy checkers are external to the study team. Checker assignment, training, detailed checklist and documented approval workflow remain R07/R09/R10 tasks. No signoff has occurred merely by adopting this rule.
