## Geography legal authority and permissions

Initial instruments are English-only, not automatically US-only. Residence questions do not establish enrollment jurisdiction, current legal age, citizenship or a provider's authorization rules. Before accepting sources/participants in a jurisdiction, the PI/institution must determine lawful consent, record transfer, cross-border storage/public release and local ethics/site requirements. If not approved, do not recruit there; retain inadvertently submitted information under institutional direction, not by inventing a legal exemption.

Onset at 6–24 months does not mean the child is still a minor when the survey occurs. Obtain the IRB's decision on current child/adult subject status and parental/legal authority for source records. Existing birthdate can support current-age checks after disclosure; any necessary pre-consent authority/age screening must be approved without naming candidate events. Parent/guardian self-attestation alone does not override required consent of an adult child or applicable permission rules. Do not request new child interaction without an amendment.
