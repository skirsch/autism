## Part A Form 1 intake

**Target and feasibility minimum:** Aim for 100 administratively complete documented-visit unique-child submissions. The 25-case minimum requires confirmed unique-child cases meeting the phenotype and 6–24-completed-month ONSET-age criteria, with contemporaneous evidence corroborating first notice (exact day or defensible bounded range) AND documentary evidence of the most recent actual vaccination on/before ONSET, with no maximum lookback. At least two reviewers must agree on the required classification, dates/bounds and corroboration status, with no material unresolved contradiction. Approximate dates may count only at their supported precision when age/anchor eligibility is established; they do not supply exact-day histogram values. Vaccine-free well-visit and no-anchor cases remain retained and may qualify for payment or other summaries, but do not count toward this minimum. Parent confirmation of no later vaccination is retained separately from documentary proof of administration; a full registry/history is not required or implied. No particular lag, vaccine product, B value or causal interpretation is required. These are distinct counts, not a statistical-power guarantee or a stop-at-25 rule; payment and approved-data release remain result-independent.

**Description:** Partners administer a two-question possible-sudden-onset/contact-interest screen for their own records, transferring authorized emails only with administrative permission/source/batch attestations. Every authorized possible-case referral receives a Survey 2 invitation without confidence screening. Research consent/authority precedes a locked 15-day unprompted narrative, neutral timing/date-confidence and records-confidence questions; named events follow only after additional disclosure and agreement. Later supporting-material requests are phased by records-confidence within the receipt-time reported phenotype/6–24-month pool. Best estimates, unknowns and out-of-range reports are retained. Three independent one-pass reviewers assess evidence, blinded to each other, not exposure. Main products are a privacy-reviewed dataset/redacted narratives and technical-validation paper; source evidence stays restricted.

**Aims:** Characterize recruitment/attrition and parent-reported developmental changes; preserve single parent-first-notice ONSET and documented timing/provenance; measure evidence availability and reviewer agreement; release an appropriately redacted, versioned resource regardless of results or journal acceptance. No causal or population-incidence estimand is proposed.

**Duration:** Start only after approvals, confirmations and prelaunch validation. Survey 2 invitations go to all authorized possible-case referrals within the approved recruitment window. Supporting-material requests operate in 14-day confidence batches; stop opening new records requests at 100 administratively complete unique-child documented-visit submissions while accepting already requested submissions. Survey 2 enrollment ceiling/end date, review/closure timetable and support remain to be confirmed separately. Public release target is 90 days from review dataset finalization. Restricted retention is 20 years from documented study closure or longer if required, not 20-year enrollment.

The overview also describes an aspirational Survey 2 parent-report yield of at least 500 otherwise qualifying parent reports if achievable. This is not a required enrollment minimum, a power calculation, or an additional reason to keep opening record-invitation batches after the 100-submission stopping threshold.

**Population:** English-understanding adult parents/legal guardians of a parent-reported autistic child. Detailed-cohort ONSET at 6–24 completed months; current child age may be older and its legal consent implications require determination. Partner aggregate screen counts are reported where available, not individual screen answers; uncertain/out-of-range study reports remain retained. No-anchor families are retained; a full vaccination history or proof of birthdate is not required.
