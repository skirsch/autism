### Section 5 Specific aims

1. Describe the response funnel, eligibility/confidence, disclosed-stage attrition and availability of supporting evidence by source.
2. Preserve reported first notice, independently reviewed dates/bounds, earliest documented post-change observations, and distinct administration/history support.
3. Record reusable measures: age at ONSET; A vaccination-to-ONSET days; B total complete normal days in that window; C calendar-quarter/fixed-age-reference lags; separate well-visit timing and contextual symptoms/history.
4. Quantify date/phenotype/B/evidence agreement and unresolved cases and validate the construction/release process.
5. Release approved public data/documentation independently of findings/journal acceptance and support controlled evidence re-review.

These are resource-characterization aims, not a confirmatory hypothesis test. Later analysis choices made after examining data are exploratory and must say so. A temporal cluster alone is not causal evidence; nominal/calendar references are not asserted valid null controls.

### Section 8 Methods

The full protocol is [study_overview.md](study_overview.md), with operational detail in the eligibility/construction, screen, dictionary, recruitment/payment and records manuals.

**Population:** Adult English-understanding respondents with appropriate research consent/legal authority; parent-reported autism. Survey 2 is available to every authorized possible-case referral, not screened by age/date confidence or record availability. The one-child rule and study-collected phenotype/6–24-month reported-age flags define the focused records pool and later scientific tiers. No seven-day regression rule; new marked behavior can qualify without skill loss; transient illness alone cannot. Unknowns/out-of-range/low-confidence reports are retained.

**Selection:** Survey 2 invitations are not confidence-selected. For later supporting-material requests, use the original neutral A4 four-group confidence answer, recorded permission and receipt-time reported phenotype/age eligibility. Initial random 100 highest-confidence candidates, filling successive groups as needed; mature requests at 14 days. Next request batch `ceil(1.20*(100-C)/(C_m/I_m))`, capped to available pool; zero yield fallback 100/remainder. Archive seeded ordering/input hash, minimize selection queue to exclude exposure/review fields, and add up to three approved sources if needed. Do not infer the administrative target ensures analytic power.

**Collection:** Single-question screens; explicit unknown and approximate dates; saved-partial retention disclosed before questions. A1 is first substantive screen and is server-locked before named events, followed by A2/A3/A4 and disclosure/continuation. Raw accounts remain immutable. Record discrete disclosure display, continue, decline and departure states; stopping is not inferred to be exposure-driven. Birthdate is entered without proof. Structured chronology covers the full eligible vaccination-to-ONSET period, including disconnected normal days and unknowns, even beyond 15 days. Collect country residence only, not addresses.

**Evidence and review:** Only existing authorized materials, uploaded securely. Metadata/provenance and recording/described-event/claimed-ONSET dates remain distinct. Three separately locked reviewers see all evidence including exposure. E0/E1/E2/E3 onset-support and V1/W1/H1 visit/history codes distinguish retrospective report, documented presence, supported first notice, actual administration and coverage. An after-only video supports documented presence but not automatically first notice. Independent exact-majority fields require consistency checks; no averaging. Unverified exact reports stay labeled. No in-window visit and parent-reported never vaccination remain retained.

**Measures:** A uses an eligible actual administration on/before ONSET within inclusive 0–90 days. B adds complete normal calendar days from vaccination through day before ONSET; vaccination day only if normal throughout. N+D+U=A when exact and reconciled; U produces B bounds rather than abnormal days. B does not equal A minus symptom duration. No vaccination means A/B not applicable. Quarter C is last calendar-quarter start; nominal C is last eligible original-birthdate 2/4/6/12/15/18-month point within 90 days, using month-end clamping. Parent, reviewer-specific and accepted derivations remain separate. No exact dates from midpoints.

**Duplicates/data completeness:** Confirm same child through restricted links/clarification, not merely same birthdate. Preserve multiple original accounts and disagreements. Unique children count once; submission funnels separately. Compensation, target count, final evidence tier and public release are independent flags. Scientific reclassification does not revise the receipt-time target; confirmed duplicates/clerical errors are auditable count corrections. All authorized submissions remain regardless of support for any hypothesis.

**Resource characterization:** Report source/batch/stage denominators, evidence/missingness/precision, duplicate/uncertain counts, three/two/unresolved agreement, administrative/payment/scientific counts, and safe suppression totals. No unobserved denominators or cessation reasons are invented. Later descriptive plots of A/B/C/age/weekday/products may be examples of use, with their own denominators, precision and selection limits. No formal causal test or SCRI/SCCS inference is promised from narrow-history selected-case data.
