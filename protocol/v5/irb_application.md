**Description:** Partners independently survey their members, retain individual answers/contacts and offer a general opt-in to relevant research invitations. They apply documented cause-neutral relevance criteria and forward our approved study link. Research consent/authority precedes study questions and direct study contact collection. A locked 15-day unprompted narrative precedes neutral timing/date-confidence and records-confidence questions, with named events disclosed afterward. Later direct materials requests are phased by confidence. Best estimates, unknowns and out-of-range reports are retained. Three independent one-pass reviewers assess evidence, blinded to each other. Main products are a privacy-reviewed dataset/redacted narratives and technical-validation paper; source evidence stays restricted. Subject to permission/privacy/IRB determination, safe partner aggregates may be compared with study and records subsets to describe selection, not to validate individuals or infer causation.

**Aims:** Characterize recruitment/attrition and parent-reported developmental changes; preserve single parent-first-notice ONSET and documented timing/provenance; measure evidence availability and reviewer agreement; release an appropriately redacted, versioned resource regardless of results or journal acceptance. No causal or population-incidence estimand is proposed.
