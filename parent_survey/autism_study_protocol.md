
# Study Protocol: Temporal Association and Clinical Sequelae in Sudden-Onset Regressive Autism

## 1. Study Objectives
To characterize the temporal and clinical sequence between vaccination, acute post-vaccination inflammatory events, and the onset of regressive autism.

## 2. Study Design
Prospective clinical case series utilizing structured intake and blinded chart adjudication.

## 3. Study Population
*   **Inclusion Criteria:** Children aged 0–5 years presenting for autism evaluation with parent-reported "sudden" or "definite" onset of regression (loss of previously acquired skills).
*   **Exclusion Criteria:** Children with a documented genetic syndrome (e.g., Fragile X, Rett) or clear evidence of regression beginning prior to the first vaccination.

## 4. Data Collection Instrument
Data will be collected at participating medical providers. Depending on the provider, the data may already be available in the medical records. In other cases, the provider will need to reach out to the parents or the child's pediatrician.

Data provided to researchers by the clinics will all be deidentified and HIPAA compliant (all dates will be converted to time intervals to prevent reidentification).

## 5. Statistical Analysis Plan
*   **Primary Outcome:** The distribution of the interval (in days) between the most recent vaccination and the onset of behavioral regression.
*   **Hypothesis:** If regression is random with respect to vaccination, onsets will be uniformly distributed between vaccination visits.
*   **Comparison:** Observed frequency of onsets in the 0–2 day and 0-7 windows compared against other windows of the same size within the interval


## 6. Ethical Considerations
*   All data collection is part of standard clinical intake.
*   The information collected on the study form will be IRB approved. 
*   Informed consent will be obtained for the use of de-identified clinical data in research.
*   The study will be conducted in accordance with the Declaration of Helsinki.

## 7. Expected Outcomes
*   Identification of an acute, post-vaccination regressive phenotype.
*   Documentation of an inflammatory clinical sequence (Vaccine → Crying/Fever/Tylenol → Regression).
*   Provision of a high-fidelity dataset that bridges the gap between parental observation and clinical documentation.

### Implementation Note for Dr. Mumper
This protocol is designed to be rigorous, clinically sound, and focused on the **acute regressive phenotype**. It avoids the pitfalls of large-scale, null-generating epidemiological studies by focusing on high-fidelity clinical data collection and verified temporal sequences.

By focusing on the **"Inflammatory Signature"** (the specific list of post-vax reactions) and the **"Sudden-Onset Phenotype,"** this protocol creates a dataset that is impossible to dismiss as "general autism." It is a study of a specific, identifiable injury process. When you share this with the clinic, emphasize that the goal is not to "find association" in the abstract, but to **document the clinical history of patients who have already regressed.**
