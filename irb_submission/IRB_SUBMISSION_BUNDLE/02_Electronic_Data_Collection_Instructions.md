## Definitions and validation

- SORA onset: first obvious, persistent ASD-relevant behavior or marked
  loss of an acquired skill, assigned by the parent/caregiver to a
  specific calendar date after previously typical development.

- Dpre = onset date − most recent vaccination date before/on onset.
  Dpost = first vaccination date after onset − onset date.

- Same-day = 0. Do not infer ordering. Do not use a routine visit
  without documented vaccine administration.

- Site code: use the nonidentifying code assigned to the clinic for this
  study. Do not enter the clinic name.

- Vaccination before onset: Yes/No based on sufficient records; never
  treat an incomplete history as No. Enter Dpre as NA when No.

- Transmit only the seven columns shown. Do not transmit names, calendar
  dates, phenotype information, MRNs/MRN-derived IDs, contact data, free
  text, geography, clinic name, or the patient mapping key.

Electronic certification accompanying upload: I verified eligibility locally. The site code is the approved study code, the row ID is not derived
from an identifier, and the clinic retains the mapping. This submission
contains only the approved fields and no calendar dates or phenotype
information. The electronic attestation metadata are stored separately
from patient-level data.
