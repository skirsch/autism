## Clinic compensation

Participating clinics may receive up to [$ amount] solely as reasonable reimbursement for documented staff time and administrative expense associated with screening, abstraction, validation, and secure electronic submission.

Payment must not be contingent upon:

- identifying a minimum number of qualifying SORA records;
- the values of Dpre or Dpost;
- finding an association;
- a particular statistical result; or
- publication or publicity.

Attach the final payment schedule, calculation method, contract, and budget justification.

## Investigator disclosures

Each investigator and key study-team member must complete the reviewing institution’s official disclosure form. At minimum, disclose:

- employment and leadership positions;
- consulting, honoraria, paid advocacy, or speaking relationships;
- equity, ownership, patents, royalties, or licensing interests;
- litigation or expert-witness roles;
- organizational, advocacy, or public positions that could reasonably be perceived to affect objectivity; and
- relationships with vaccine manufacturers, vaccine-policy organizations, autism-service organizations, MAPS, participating clinics, or study funders.

## Management measures

- eligibility is determined before timing values are examined;
- the statistical analysis plan is prespecified;
- clinic payments are effort-based and outcome-independent;
- a qualified independent statistician reviews the analysis;
- funding and interests are disclosed in presentations, datasets, and manuscripts; and
- additional measures required by [institutional COI committee] will be followed.

The funder’s roles were [state each role]. Author disclosures are [complete].”
