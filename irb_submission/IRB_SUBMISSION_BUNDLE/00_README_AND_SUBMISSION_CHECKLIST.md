# SORA IRB Submission Bundle

This directory contains the editable study documents and attachments for an IRB submission concerning the temporal relationship between vaccination and sudden-onset regressive autism (SORA).

## Submit these study documents

- `01_SORA_IRB_Submission_Packet.md` — master protocol, statistical analysis plan, privacy/security plan, waiver rationale, instrument description, conflicts, and references.
- `02_Electronic_Data_Collection_Instructions.md` — human-readable instructions for clinics.
- `03_SORA_Electronic_Data_Submission_Template.xlsx` — electronic instrument clinics will complete and upload. Paper submission is not permitted.
- `04_Clinic_Recruitment_Materials.md` — clinic email and phone script. Marked not for use until IRB approval/determination.
- `05_Waiver_Request.md` — stand-alone waiver language for an IRB portal or attachment.
- `06_Data_Privacy_and_Clinic_Attestation.md` — data-flow description and clinic certification.
- `07_Funding_Compensation_and_COI_Disclosure.md` — funding, clinic compensation, and conflict disclosures.
- `08_Investigator_and_Site_Information.md` — PI, study-team, institution, and site details.
- `09_Submission_Items_Obtained_Outside_This_Bundle.md` — documents that must come from Liz, MAPS, participating institutions, or the reviewing IRB.

## Before submitting

Search all Markdown files for `[` and `TBD`. Resolve every bracketed placeholder that applies. Ensure the study title, PI affiliation, reviewing institution, secure upload system, storage platform, retention period, funding terms, clinic compensation, statistician, and protocol dates agree across all documents.

The reviewing IRB’s online application remains controlling. Copy answers from these drafts into its required fields and upload the requested attachments. Do not begin clinic recruitment or data collection until the IRB has issued a written determination and all site/privacy requirements are satisfied.
