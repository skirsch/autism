## Current v5 study development

The current data-resource/IRB working package is indexed in [protocol/v5/submission_checklist.md](protocol/v5/submission_checklist.md). The canonical [IPAKIRB Form 2 proposal](irb_submission/IPAKIRB_Form_2_v5.md) follows the supplied template and includes earlier-feedback responses, proposed participant text and unresolved TBDs. Partners retain their member-survey answers and contact lists, apply documented relevance criteria and forward approved study invitations. Our consent precedes research questions and direct contact collection; confidence phases only later evidence requests. Safe aggregate stage comparisons are subject to privacy, permission and oversight determination. The design uses one parent-first-notice ONSET and distinct A/B/C measures. It is not approved for recruitment. Historical discussion below is separate from this protocol and is not a v5 causal finding.

### Autism parent survey
I surveyed over 12,000 parents on my Substack on May 19, 2023 and asked about how many vaccines their kids got and what their symptoms were.

The odds ratio for autism caused by vaccines ranged from 2 to 3.5. The more vaccines, the higher the OR value.

This survey could be repeated by any researcher, but it's clear that that will never happen, isn't it?

The "vitamin K" shot is also VERY detrimental.
