### Autism parent survey
I surveyed over 12,000 parents on my Substack on May 19, 2023 and asked about how many vaccines their kids got and what their symptoms were.

The odds ratio for autism caused by vaccines ranged from 2 to 3.5. The more vaccines, the higher the OR value.

This survey could be repeated by any researcher, but it's clear that that will never happen, isn't it?
